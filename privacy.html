# LogicRoomX Press Privacy Policy **Last Updated: November 11, 2026** ## 1. Introduction LogicRoomX Press ("we", "our", or "us") is an independent publishing company specializing in large-print word search puzzle books for adults and seniors. We operate a private internal publishing tool that uses the official Pinterest API to manage and publish promotional content on our own Pinterest Business account. This tool is developed exclusively for our internal marketing activities and is not provided to third-party users. ## 2. Information We Access and Process Our internal publishing tool may access and process the following information solely for its intended publishing functions: **Pinterest Account Information** - Basic account information made available through the Pinterest API. - The list of boards belonging to our authorized Pinterest Business account. - Relevant board identifiers required to select publishing destinations. **Publishing Content** - Original promotional images and videos created by LogicRoomX Press. - Pin titles and descriptions. - Destination URLs linking to our published book listings. - The selected Pinterest board and scheduled publishing information. **Authorization Information** The application uses Pinterest OAuth 2.0 to obtain authorization for API access. We do not request, collect, or store Pinterest account passwords or session cookies. ## 3. How We Use Information Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used exclusively for our internal publishing workflow, including: - Connecting to our own authorized Pinterest Business account. - Retrieving our own board information to select publishing destinations. - Uploading original promotional videos and images. - Creating individual Pins after explicit review and confirmation by our authorized operator. - Managing the status of individually scheduled publishing tasks. We do not use Pinterest information for unrelated purposes, third-party advertising, data resale, or the development or training of AI models. ## 4. Data Storage and Security OAuth access credentials are stored in a restricted local environment controlled by LogicRoomX Press and are not made publicly accessible. We retain only the minimum operational information necessary to manage publishing tasks explicitly selected by our authorized operator. Pinterest account, board, and Pin information is accessed only as needed for the requested API operations. We do not build or maintain a separate database of Pinterest account information for unrelated purposes. We take reasonable measures to protect authorization credentials and publishing information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. ## 5. Information Sharing We do not sell, rent, or commercially share Pinterest account information with third parties. When a Pin is explicitly approved for publication, the relevant promotional content and publishing information are transmitted to Pinterest through its official API. Pinterest processes this information in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable terms. Our public information website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information associated with website access under its own privacy policies. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion We retain authorization credentials only for as long as necessary to operate the internal publishing tool. Publishing task information is retained only as needed to complete, manage, or cancel the corresponding publishing task. We remove information that is no longer necessary for the intended purpose. Access to our Pinterest account can be revoked through Pinterest's account authorization settings. Locally stored authorization credentials can also be deleted when the application is no longer in use. ## 7. Third-Party Services Our application interacts with Pinterest through its official API. Users who visit our publishing website or follow links to Amazon book listings may be subject to the respective third-party services' privacy policies and terms. We do not operate or control the privacy practices of these external services. ## 8. Children's Privacy Our publishing tool is intended exclusively for internal business use and is not designed for or directed toward children under the age of 13. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when our application or data handling practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date. ## 10.
