Privacy Policy

How LogicRoomX Press handles information in connection with its internal Pinterest publishing tool.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

LogicRoomX Press ("we," "our," or "us") is an independent publishing company specializing in large-print word search puzzle books for adults and seniors.

We are developing a private internal publishing tool that uses the official Pinterest API to manage promotional content on our own Pinterest Business account. The tool is intended solely for our internal business use and is not offered to third-party users.

2. Information the Tool May Access

When authorized and when needed for a requested publishing task, the tool may access or process:

  • Basic Pinterest account information made available to the application through the authorized API scopes.
  • Board names and identifiers from our own authorized Pinterest Business account, so an operator can select a publishing destination.
  • Pin identifiers or status information when needed to check the result of a publishing operation.
  • Promotional images and videos, Pin titles and descriptions, destination URLs, selected boards, and publishing instructions supplied by our team.
  • OAuth authorization tokens required to make API requests that our account has authorized.

The exact information accessed depends on the API permissions granted and the features we use.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only to operate our internal publishing workflow, including to connect to our authorized account, display our available boards, prepare promotional Pins, submit approved content to Pinterest, and manage the status of those publishing tasks.

Each Pin is intended to be individually selected and reviewed by an authorized operator before it is submitted for publication. We do not use Pinterest information for unrelated purposes, sell it, or provide a service that lets third parties access Pinterest accounts through our application.

4. Authorization

The application uses Pinterest's OAuth 2.0 authorization process and requests only the API permissions needed for its functions.

We do not ask users to provide their Pinterest passwords, and we do not intentionally collect Pinterest session cookies. Authorization is granted through Pinterest's own authorization flow.

5. Storage and Security

We aim to retain only the minimum information needed to carry out and manage our internal publishing tasks. Authorization tokens and operational information must be kept in a restricted environment controlled by LogicRoomX Press, protected against public access and unauthorized use.

We do not place application secrets, access tokens, or refresh tokens in this public website or in a publicly accessible source-code repository. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent Pinterest information. When an operator approves a Pin for publication, the relevant content and publishing details are transmitted to Pinterest through its official API so Pinterest can perform the requested action.

Our public information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with website requests under its own terms and privacy policies. Links to Amazon or other third-party services are subject to those services' own privacy practices.

7. Retention and Deletion

We intend to keep authorization tokens only while they are needed to operate the authorized integration. Operational records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the related publishing tasks and maintain basic business records.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or securely remove it from the systems under our control, subject to any applicable legal recordkeeping requirements. Authorization can also be revoked through the relevant Pinterest account settings. Revoking authorization prevents future API access using that authorization.

8. Children's Privacy

The internal publishing tool is a business tool and is not designed for children or intended to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if our application, business processes, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
