LogicRoomX Press Privacy Policy

LogicRoomX Press ("we") operates an internal tool that uses the Pinterest API to create and schedule Pins on our own Pinterest Business account, for the purpose of promoting our large-print word search puzzle books.

We do not collect, store, or share personal data from any third party. The Pinterest account credentials used by this tool belong to LogicRoomX Press and are stored securely. We do not share any data with third parties.
