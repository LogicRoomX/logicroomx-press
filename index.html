# LogicRoomX Press **Independent Publishing | Large-Print Word Search Puzzle Books** ## About Us LogicRoomX Press is an independent publishing company specializing in large-print word search puzzle books for adults and seniors. We create and publish original word search puzzle books through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP), with a focus on enjoyable, accessible, and easy-to-read puzzle experiences. ## Our Pinterest Publishing Tool LogicRoomX Press is developing a private internal publishing tool to manage promotional content for our books on our own Pinterest Business account. The tool is designed exclusively for our internal marketing activities. It uses the official Pinterest API and OAuth 2.0 authorization to connect to our own Pinterest Business account. Our intended workflow includes: - Uploading original promotional images and videos for our published books. - Selecting the Pinterest board for each individual Pin. - Preparing Pin titles, descriptions, and links to the corresponding Amazon book listings. - Reviewing and explicitly confirming each individual Pin before publication. - Scheduling approved Pins for publication at selected times. Every Pin is individually selected and approved by our authorized operator. The tool does not publish content or perform account actions without explicit authorization.
