Who we are

Publishing with readability in mind.

We create and publish word search puzzle books through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP). Our books focus on large-print layouts and approachable puzzle experiences for readers who enjoy a calm, engaging activity.

Our work brings together puzzle content, practical page design, and book presentation to make it easy for readers to discover their next puzzle.
